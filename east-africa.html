East Africa

East African cooking mixes highland farming traditions with coastal trade influences.

Staples

Teff, maize, beans, plantains, cassava leaves and sweet potatoes are common. Ethiopian kitchens rely on the spice blend berbere, while Rwandan cooking leans on groundnuts and greens.

Cooking techniques

Fermenting batter for injera, steaming plantains in banana leaves and slow simmering stews are all classic methods in the region.

Dishes to try

  • Injera: Soft, tangy flatbread made from fermented teff batter.
  • Isombe: Rwandan cassava leaves roasted, simmered with meat and finished with ground peanuts.
