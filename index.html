Latest food trends

Ancient grains are back

Cooks everywhere are rediscovering teff, millet and fonio for their flavor and nutrition.

Street food goes global

Suya, mandazi and kachumbari are showing up on menus far from home as chefs bring West and East African flavors to new audiences.

Plant-forward African cooking

Dishes built on beans, greens and groundnuts, like isombe, are a natural fit for people eating less meat.
