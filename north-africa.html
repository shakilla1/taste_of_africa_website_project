North Africa

North African cuisine blends Berber, Arab and Mediterranean traditions into rich, aromatic dishes.

Staples

Couscous, wheat, olives, dates, chickpeas and preserved lemons are everywhere. The spice shelf includes cumin, harissa and ras el hanout.

Cooking techniques

Slow cooking in a clay tagine keeps meat tender and lets sweet and savory flavors blend. Couscous is steamed above the stew so it soaks up the steam.

Dishes to try

  • Lamb Tagine: Slow-cooked lamb with prunes, almonds and warm spices.
