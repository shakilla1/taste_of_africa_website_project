Lamb Tagine

A tagine is both the dish and the cone-shaped clay pot it cooks in. This version is a Mrouzia-style lamb tagine, slow cooked until tender in a thick sauce of cumin, ginger and cinnamon with sweet dried fruit and almonds. It shows the mix of savory spices and sweet fruit that Moroccan cooking is known for.

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb, cut into pieces
  • 0.5 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 0.5 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 0.25 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika, for the lamb
  • 0.5 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • A pinch of turmeric
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped or grated
  • 0.25 teaspoon paprika, for the onion
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 4.5 tablespoons saffron water (a pinch of saffron in a little hot water)
  • 2 medium tomatoes, grated
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 1 cup hot water, enough to cover the lamb
  • 6 dried apricots, soaked in hot water
  • 6 prunes, soaked in hot water
  • 0.25 cup raisins, soaked in hot water
  • 2 dried figs, halved and soaked in hot water
  • 0.25 cup almonds, soaked and peeled
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 0.5 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • A small piece of butter
  • Salt, to taste

Cooking instructions

Show the steps
  1. Mix the lamb with the cumin, ginger, black pepper, coriander, turmeric, paprika, cayenne and salt. Leave it to marinate for 30 minutes.
  2. Soak the apricots, prunes, raisins and figs in hot water for 20 minutes, then drain them.
  3. Heat the olive oil in the tagine, set on a heat diffuser or heavy pan. Cook the onion with a pinch of salt and the extra paprika on high heat.
  4. Add the garlic and mix well, then add the marinated lamb. Stir on high heat for 3 to 4 minutes, until the color changes.
  5. Add the saffron water and the grated tomatoes and stir. Cover and cook on high heat for 7 to 10 minutes, until the mixture starts bubbling.
  6. Add the bay leaf, cinnamon stick and just enough hot water to cover the lamb. Stir, cover, and cook on medium-low heat for 1 to 2 hours. The longer, the better.
  7. While the lamb cooks, melt the butter in a pan. Add the fruit, honey and ground cinnamon and stir on medium-low heat until the fruit is caramelized. Set it aside.
  8. Fry the almonds in the same pan for 2 to 3 minutes, until golden. Set them aside.
  9. When the lamb is tender and the sauce is thick, add the fruit and almonds. If the sauce is still watery, reduce it on high heat first. Simmer for another 5 minutes.
  10. Serve with couscous, crusty bread or a Moroccan spiced bulgur salad.
