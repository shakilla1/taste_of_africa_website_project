West Africa

West African food is bold, generous and built around big flavors. Meals are made to be shared.

Staples

Rice, yams, plantains, cassava and millet make up the base of most meals. Flavor comes from scotch bonnet peppers, ginger, onions, tomatoes and palm oil.

Cooking techniques

Cooks simmer thick stews and one-pot rice dishes slowly so the flavors deepen. Grilling over open flames, like suya skewers, is also a big part of street food.

Dishes to try

  • Jollof Rice: Smoky one-pot rice cooked in a rich tomato and pepper base.
