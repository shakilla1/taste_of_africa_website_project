Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is the pride of West African kitchens. Rice is simmered in a sauce of tomatoes, peppers and onion until every grain turns deep red and a little smoky. Nigerians, Ghanaians and Senegalese all claim the best version, and the debate is part of the fun.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice, washed
  • 0.75 medium bell pepper, for the blended mix
  • 1.25 Roma tomatoes, for the blended mix
  • 0.5 scotch bonnet pepper, for the blended mix
  • A small piece of onion, for the blended mix
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 0.25 large red onion, chopped
  • 0.56 cup tomato paste
  • 0.5 chicken seasoning cube
  • 1.5 teaspoons thyme
  • 0.75 teaspoon curry powder
  • A pinch of garlic powder
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1.5 teaspoons salt
  • 0.63 cup chicken stock (or water)
  • 0.25 cup water, plus enough to cover the surface of the rice

Cooking instructions

Show the steps
  1. Wash the rice and set it aside.
  2. Blend the bell pepper, tomato, scotch bonnet and the small piece of onion until smooth.
  3. Pour the blended mix into an empty pot and cook it until the water dries off.
  4. In a separate pot, heat the oil and fry the chopped red onion for 5 to 8 minutes, until fragrant.
  5. Add the tomato paste and fry it for about 20 minutes.
  6. Stir in the boiled-down pepper and tomato mix and fry for another 8 to 10 minutes.
  7. Add the seasoning cube, thyme, curry, garlic powder, bay leaf and salt, and stir well.
  8. Pour in the chicken stock or water, then add the washed rice and mix.
  9. Add just enough water to cover the surface of the rice, cover the pot with foil and cook for 25 minutes.
  10. Open the pot, mix the rice and serve.
